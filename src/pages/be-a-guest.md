This podcast only works because people trust us with their stories. If you have lived experience of disability, or you work in disability support and have something honest to say about how the system works, we want to hear from you.

## Who we're looking for

- People with disability with a story to tell, whatever it is
- Family members and carers
- Support workers, coordinators and allied health professionals
- Providers and advocates who want to talk honestly about the NDIS

## What to expect

A relaxed recorded conversation, not an interview grilling. You see the questions beforehand. Nothing goes out without your OK, and we accommodate whatever you need to take part comfortably, in whatever format works for you.
